Masculine & feminine nouns (o / a)

Every Spanish noun has a gender. It mostly follows the last letter: -o → masculine, -a → feminine. Articles and adjectives agree with the noun.

singularplural
elel carro rojolos carros rojos
lala casa bonitalas casas bonitas
  • Nouns in -o are usually masculine: el libro, el vaso, el boleto.
  • Nouns in -a are usually feminine: la mesa, la cuenta, la playa.
  • Exceptions worth memorising: la mano, la foto, el día, el mapa, el problema.
Tip: learn every noun with its article — la cuenta, never just cuenta.

In Mexico you'll hear el camión for the city bus and el carro for car (not coche so much).

When in doubt, say it anyway — a wrong article never stops anyone from understanding you.
